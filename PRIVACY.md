# Fatto Privacy Policy

**Effective date: September 25, 2026**

Fatto is an open-source taskwarrior client for Android. The app does not send analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports to the developer.

## Information stored on your device

Fatto stores your task data and app settings locally on your device. Sync configuration and credentials that you enter are stored using Android encrypted preferences. Notifications, when enabled, are scheduled by the app on your device.

## Optional synchronization

Fatto does not provide a hosted sync service or send your tasks to a Fatto operated server. If you configure synchronization, the app sends and receives task data using the TaskChampion sync server or S3-compatible storage endpoint that you choose. The sync provider receives the information necessary to perform that synchronization. Its handling, retention, and security practices are governed by that provider's policies. You can use Fatto without configuring sync.

## Information shared with the developer

Fatto does not include analytics or crash-reporting services. The developer does not receive task data or sync credentials through the app. If you contact the developer, the information you choose to include in your message will be received to respond to your request.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The current version will be available at this URL https://github.com/brokenpip3/fatto/blob/main/PRIVACY.md
